A FEW LITTLE THINGS TO KNOW

Before you
begin.

Can I see the invitation before I choose?

Yes. Every design has a full interactive preview. Open a design from the collection to experience it before personalising.

Can I change the words and photographs?

Yes. Use the guided steps for your event details, then the invitation editor for individual words and images.

How do my guests reply?

Once your invitation is paid and published, share its guest link. Your guests can RSVP from the invitation, and you can view responses in your dashboard.

Can I come back to my draft?

Your draft saves on the device you’re using. After you save it to your account, you can return to it from your dashboard.
